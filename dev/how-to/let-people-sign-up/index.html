Let people sign up¶

By default only people you invite can have an account on your website. You can also let anyone create their own account. They confirm their email address with a 6-digit code before they can sign in.

You need to be an admin.

Turn sign-up on¶

  1. Choose Site settings in the admin menu, then Change who can sign up.
  2. Tick Let anyone create an account.
  3. In Role of people who sign up, keep Member unless you have a reason to change it. Members can read members-only pages and nothing more. Only roles that are safe to give to people you don't know are listed: never editor or admin.
  4. Choose Save sign-up settings. You should see "The sign-up settings have been saved."

The sign-in page now shows "New here?" with a Create an account link.

Try it as a new person¶

  1. Sign out, or open a private browser window, and go to the sign-in page, for example http://localhost:8080/login.
  2. Choose Create an account. Fill in your first name, last name, an optional display name, your email address, your time zone (your browser's is already chosen) and a password. On a website in several languages, choose your language too.
  3. Choose Create my account. You should see the heading Confirm your email address.
  4. Open the email. While you try SightPress on your computer, it is in the test inbox: see Open the test e-mail inbox. It has a 6-digit code and no link.
  5. Type the code in 6-digit code and choose Confirm my email address. You should see "Your email address is confirmed and your account is ready."
  6. Sign in with your email address and password.

The code works once, for 15 minutes. Send a new code sends another one, at most once a minute. See Codes we send you.

What keeps it safe¶

  • An account can't sign in until its email address is confirmed. Accounts that are never confirmed are removed after a week.
  • If someone signs up with an address that already has an account, the page looks exactly the same, so nobody can find out who has an account. The owner of the address gets an email saying someone tried, with no link.
  • A hidden field catches most automated sign-ups, and each place can send at most 10 sign-up forms an hour. There are no picture puzzles.
  • Every new account and every change to these settings is written to the Audit log.

To turn sign-up off again, untick Let anyone create an account. Accounts that were already made are kept.
