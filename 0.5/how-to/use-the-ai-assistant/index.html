Use the AI assistant¶

The AI assistant suggests text for your website: summaries, descriptions for search engines, alt text for pictures, drafts of pages and translations. It also answers questions about using SightPress. It uses an AI service you choose, with your own key from that service, so you pay the service directly for what you use.

The assistant only ever suggests. Nothing it writes is used until a person inserts it and saves. Alt text and translations it writes are marked until a person has checked them, and pages that use them can't be published before that.

The AI assistant is an optional feature, off for new websites. Nothing is sent to any AI service until an admin sets it up and someone asks for a suggestion.

Before you start¶

  • You need to be an admin of the website to turn the assistant on and set it up.
  • You need an API key from one of the AI services SightPress works with: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), Mistral AI, or a service or your own server that works like OpenAI's. Each service's website explains how to get a key and what it costs. Check where the service processes data before you choose it: Mistral AI, for example, processes it in the EU.

Turn the assistant on¶

  1. Sign in to the admin panel, for example at http://localhost:8080/admin.
  2. In the admin menu, choose Features.
  3. Find AI assistant and choose Turn on AI assistant.
  4. You should see that its status is now "On". A new screen appears in the admin menu under Site settings: AI assistant.

See Turn features on or off for more about features.

Choose the service and enter your key¶

  1. In the admin menu, choose Site settings, then AI assistant.
  2. Under Status you should see "Not ready yet", followed by what is missing.
  3. Under AI service, choose a service. Each one says what it is.
  4. Choose a Model. Bigger models write better and cost more. For an OpenAI-compatible service, type the Model name and the Address of the service instead (it must start with https://).
  5. Paste your key into New API key and choose Save the AI service.
  6. You should see "The AI service is saved." Under Status it now says "Ready", and the page shows only the last four characters of your key, for example "Saved API key: ••••1234". The key is stored encrypted and never shown again. To change it, paste a new one; to delete it, choose Remove key.
  7. Choose Test connection. You should see "The connection works", or what went wrong (for example a key the service didn't accept).

If your organisation chooses the AI service for every website, this screen shows its choice and you can't change it.

Choose who may use it, and a budget¶

  1. On the same screen, under Who may use it, and how much, tick the roles that may ask the assistant. Admins always may.
  2. In Monthly budget in tokens, enter how many tokens (pieces of words, sent and received) the website may use each month. Leave it empty to use your organisation's budget; enter 0 for no limit.
  3. Choose Save who may use it. The screen shows how much has been used this month.

Months start on the 1st. From 80% of the budget, everyone who asks is warned; at 100% the assistant stops until the next month. Each person can ask 20 times every 10 minutes.

Ask a question¶

  1. On the Dashboard (or at the end of a page's editor), find Ask the assistant.
  2. Type your question in Your question, for example "How do I add a page to the main menu?", and choose Ask.
  3. You should hear or see "Working on it…", then "Suggestion ready". The answer appears under its own heading, which gets focus. It ends with a link to the part of this documentation it used.

The assistant only tells you about screens your role can use. If something needs an admin, it says so.

On a page's editor you can tick Share this page with the assistant to ask about the page itself. It reads the saved page, so save your changes first.

Get a suggestion for a field¶

On a page's editor, under Summary, Title in search results and Description for search engines, there is a Suggest button.

  1. Save the page first: the assistant reads the saved page.
  2. Choose, for example, Suggest a summary.
  3. The suggestion appears in Ask the assistant, under its own heading, which gets focus.
  4. Choose Insert to put it in the field (focus goes back to the field), Copy to copy it, Try again for another one, or Discard.
  5. Check the text, change it if needed, and choose Save page.

Without JavaScript, the same buttons open a page with the suggestion; Insert takes you back to the field with the text in it.

Rewrite text, draft a page, or explain a check¶

  • Rewrite text (in every Ask the assistant): paste some text, choose Make it simpler, shorter, friendlier or more formal, and choose Rewrite. Copy the result into your page.
  • Draft this page (on a page's editor): say what the page should be about and choose Draft this page. Choose Add to the end of the page: the headings and text are added to the page's blocks, not yet saved. Things to check or fill in are in [square brackets].
  • Explain this (next to each publish check on a page's editor): the assistant explains what the check means and how to fix it.

Alt text for pictures¶

The assistant can describe pictures only with a model that can see pictures (most listed models can).

  1. In the admin menu, choose Media and open a picture.
  2. Under the alt text field, choose Suggest alt text.
  3. Choose Insert, check the text, and choose Save details.
  4. You should see "The AI assistant suggested this alt text." Pages that show this text can't be published until someone checks it.
  5. When the text describes the picture well, tick I checked the alt text: it describes the picture and choose Save details. Changing the text and saving it counts as checking it too.

Draft a translation¶

On a website in more than one language:

  1. Open a page's editor. In the Translations section, choose Draft a translation into followed by the language, with the AI assistant.
  2. The new translation opens in the editor, with a notice: "The AI assistant drafted this translation, and nobody has checked it yet." Its blocks, pictures and links are the same as the original's; only the words are translated.
  3. Read it and change what needs changing. Then save the page, or choose I checked this translation.
  4. Only then can the translation be published. Until then the list of pages and the page's older versions say "drafted by the AI assistant, not checked yet".

What is sent, and what is kept¶

  • Sent to the AI service: your question, and the page or picture you ask a suggestion for. Never lists of people, email addresses, the audit log or other pages.
  • Kept on your website: when the assistant was used, by whom, for what, with which service and model, and how many tokens. Never the question, the page text or the answer.
  • The AI service handles what it receives under its own terms.

Turn the assistant off¶

On the Features screen, choose Turn off AI assistant. The panel, the Suggest buttons and the AI assistant screen disappear. The settings, the key and the record of what was used are kept, so turning it on again brings everything back.

From the command line¶

Whoever runs your SightPress installation can set the assistant up with site:assistant:set, test it with site:assistant:test, see what was used with site:assistant:usage and turn it off with site:assistant:disable. See the command line reference.
