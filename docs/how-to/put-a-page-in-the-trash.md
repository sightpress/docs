# Put a page in the trash and get it back

You can take a page off the website in two ways, and both keep it so you can bring it back:

- **Archive** keeps the page out of sight. Visitors who open its address are told the page isn't found.
- **Put in the trash** is for pages you want to get rid of. Visitors who open its address are told the page is
  **gone**, so search engines drop it. A page stays in the trash until someone deletes it for good.

You need to be an **editor** or **admin**.

## Archive a page

1. Choose **Pages** in the admin menu, then the page.
2. Choose **Archive**. You should see "Archived. Visitors can’t see the page any more; it is kept with the archived
   pages."
3. To bring it back, open it again and choose **Restore as a draft**. It comes back as a draft: publish it again when
   it is ready.

## Put a page in the trash

1. Open the page in the editor.
2. Choose **Put in the trash**. You should see "Put in the trash. Its address now tells visitors the page is gone."

A page with pages under it can only go in the trash once those pages are in the trash already, so nothing is left
without a page above it.

## Find a page in the trash and get it back

1. Choose **Pages** in the admin menu.
2. In the **State** filter, choose **In the trash**, then choose **Show pages**. You should see only the pages in the trash.
3. Open the page and choose **Take out of the trash**. You should see "Restored as a draft. Publish it again when it
   is ready."

A page comes out of the trash as a draft, and only when the page above it isn't in the trash.

## Delete a page for good

This can't be undone.

1. Open a page that is in the trash.
2. Choose **Delete this page for good**. A page asks you to confirm, and lists what goes with it: the pages under it,
   every translation and every older version.
3. Choose **Delete for good** to go ahead, or **Keep it in the trash** to go back.

Its address then tells visitors and search engines that the page is gone, until a new page uses the address. Menu
entries that pointed to it are removed.

## Copy a page

**Copy this page** makes a new draft right after the page, in every language it is written in, titled "Copy of …",
with `-copy` added to its address. The pages under it aren't copied. The copy opens in the editor.

Every step on this page is written to the website’s audit log, which admins can read.
