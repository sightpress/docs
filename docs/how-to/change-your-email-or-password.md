# Change your email address or password

Everyone with an account on a website, members included, can change their own details on **My account**.

## Open My account

Sign in, then choose **My account**: in the admin panel it is a link in the header; on the website, go to `/account`,
for example <http://localhost:8080/account>.

On *My account* you can also change your name, display name, time zone, language (on websites in several languages)
and profile picture. People who use the admin panel can choose **Reduce motion** and **Larger text** there.

## Change your password

1. On *My account*, choose **Change your password**.
2. In **Current password**, type the password you use now.
3. In **New password**, type a new one. The rules are written under the field.
4. Choose **Change password**. You should see "Your password has been changed. You are still signed in here, and
   signed out everywhere else."

If the current password is wrong, a message at the top of the page says so. After five wrong tries in 15 minutes,
you have to wait, as when signing in.

## Change your email address

1. On *My account*, choose **Change your email address**.
2. Type the **New email address** and your **Current password**, then choose **Send the code**.
3. We email a 6-digit code to the **new** address. The email has no link.
4. Type the code on the next page and choose **Confirm my new email address**. You should see "Your email address is
   now …. Sign in with it from now on."

Your old address gets an email saying that the address was changed, with the new one partly hidden and no link.
Until you type the code, nothing changes. See [Codes we send you](codes-we-send-you.md).

Every change is written to the website's audit log, without the passwords.
