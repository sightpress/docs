# Get notifications by text

Some notifications that a website sends by email can also come by text message, if you choose. The email always comes
too.

| Notification | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| *When a page is sent to you for review* | Someone sends a page for review and you can publish it. |

## Choose them

1. [Add your mobile number](add-your-mobile-number.md) if you haven't.
2. On **My account → Mobile number and text messages**, tick **Send me text messages**.
3. Tick the notifications you want, then choose **Save my choices**. You should see "You will get the text messages
   you chose."

## What a text looks like

> Riverside Library: a page is waiting for your review. Sign in to the admin panel to see it.

Texts are short and in your language. They never contain the page's title or anything else from the page, and never a
link: sign in to the website yourself to see what happened.

Texts are sent within a minute or two. If your organisation's monthly budget for texts is used up, notifications stop
until next month, but emails keep coming.

## Stop them

Untick **Send me text messages**, remove your number, or reply **STOP** to any of our texts. Reply **START** to get
them again.

## For admins

**Site settings → Text messages** shows how much of the month's budget is used and the latest messages, with numbers
partly hidden and without their text. **Send a test message** checks that texts arrive.
