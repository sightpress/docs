# Test with assistive technology

If you use a screen reader, a magnifier, a braille display, voice control or switch access, you can tell us how
SightPress works with it before each release. Your experience matters more than any automatic test.

## Before each release

Before each new version, we post a **Testers wanted** discussion on
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/sightpress/core/discussions). It lists the new and changed screens, and how
to try them. Reply there to say you're taking part, or email <contribute@sightpress.org>.

## How to test

1. Run SightPress on your own computer, as in [Getting started](../getting-started.md), and create a website.
2. Go through the screens listed in the discussion, with the technology you use every day, in the way you normally
   work. For example: sign in, write and publish a page, translate it, search the website, read the analytics.
3. Note anything that stops you, slows you down or surprises you: something not announced, announced twice or
   announced wrongly; focus that jumps or gets lost; a control you can't reach or operate; a chart or table you can't
   understand.
4. Note what worked well too. It tells us what to keep.

We test ourselves with NVDA and Firefox on Windows and VoiceOver and Safari on macOS. Other combinations, such as
JAWS, Narrator, TalkBack, VoiceOver on iOS, Orca, ZoomText or Dragon, are especially welcome.

## Report what you found

For each problem, [report an accessibility barrier](report-a-problem.md): the task, your assistive technology and its
version, your browser and operating system, what happened and what you expected. Accessibility barriers are always
treated as bugs.

You never need to explain or prove a disability to take part.
