# Sign in with your phone

On websites with [text messages](set-up-text-messages.md), you can sign in with your confirmed mobile number instead
of your email address. You still need your password.

## Sign in

1. [Add your mobile number](add-your-mobile-number.md) and confirm it with the code, if you haven't.
2. On the sign-in page, type your number in **Email address or mobile number**, the way you usually write it
   (`07700 900123`) or with `+` and the country code.
3. Type your password and choose **Sign in**.

Only a confirmed number works. After you change your number, the old one stops working at once. If you get "Enter a
correct email address or mobile number, and password.", check both and try again. After 5 wrong tries, wait 15
minutes: tries with your email address and with your number count together.

## Forgot your password?

1. Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Type your mobile number and choose **Send me a code**. The code comes by text and works for 10 minutes. It never
   contains a link.
3. Type the code, then choose a new password.

We then send an email to your address saying your password was changed after a code to your phone. If it wasn't you,
reset your password with your email address straight away and tell the website's admins.

People who manage the website (users, roles, settings, features or security) can only reset by email, and so can
everyone while the website [asks for a code for important changes](confirm-important-changes.md). That way a stolen or
recycled phone number is not enough to take over an account.
