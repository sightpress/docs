# Choose a page layout

A **layout** decides how a page's content is arranged. You choose one for each page. Your words and blocks stay the
same when you change it.

1. In the admin menu, choose **Pages**, then open a page, or choose to add a new one.
2. Find **Layout** and choose one.
3. Save the page, then open it to check how it reads.

| Layout | What it does | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home page** | The title and summary as a large introduction. | The first page of the website. |
| **Standard page** | One comfortable reading column. | Most pages. |
| **Landing** | A wide introduction band, then content in full-width sections. | A single campaign or call to action. |
| **Sidebar** | Content with a side column of related links and notes. It lists the pages in the same section. | A section with several related pages. |
| **Full width** | Content across the whole page width. | Wide galleries and tables. |

On narrow screens and when zoomed in, every layout becomes one column, so nothing needs sideways scrolling.

## When you change theme

Not every theme has every layout. Before you switch to a theme that lacks one, the **Appearance** screen lists the
pages affected. They are shown in the standard layout, and they keep their own layout for when you switch back. See
[Install and change a theme](install-and-change-a-theme.md).
